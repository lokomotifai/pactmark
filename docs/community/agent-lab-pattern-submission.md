---
title: Agent Lab pattern submission
description: Submit reusable agent patterns with bounded evidence and maturity claims.
---

> Compatibility: Pactmark 0.1.x.

A Komünite Agent Lab submission includes the exact agent, tool, policy, verifier,
schema, model-profile, and environment identities; representative work orders;
artifacts and redacted evidence; success and denial tests; known failures; security
review; and the proposed maturity level.

Do not submit credentials, customer data, hidden reasoning, unverifiable screenshots,
or claims of universal quality. Repeated use and maintainer review may promote a
pattern; the submitting model or author cannot self-certify it.
