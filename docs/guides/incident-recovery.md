---
title: Incident recovery
description: Contain authority, preserve append-only evidence, and reconcile uncertain effects.
---

> Compatibility: Pactmark 0.2.x.

First contain access: revoke credentials and grants, activate digest kill switches,
stop workers or egress where required, and preserve tenant boundaries. Do not delete
or rewrite run truth during diagnosis.

Classify each effect as acknowledged, definitely not dispatched, or uncertain. Only
the registered strategy may prove retry, perform lookup reconciliation, or start a
separately authorized compensation `WorkOrder`. Human guesses are not receipts.

Rotate exposed secrets, rebuild projections, restore protected stores if required,
verify artifact/evidence bindings, notify affected owners, and record timeline,
scope, residual risk, and follow-up tests. Use the repository incident playbook and
security reporting channel.
